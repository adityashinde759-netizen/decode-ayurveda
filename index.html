Critical Thinking & Clarity

Not everything you hear about Ayurveda is Ayurveda.

Comparing modern viral wellness marketing against direct Sanskrit passages from the classical compendia.

close The Popular Myth

“Ayurveda is just grandmother’s herbal home remedies with zero actual medical structure.”

Social media portrays Ayurveda as merely drinking turmeric milk, sipping hot water, and swallowing random herbal powders without diagnostics or physiological rationale.

Status: Gross Simplification & Mischaracterization
check What Classical Texts Actually State

Classical Ayurveda is an 8-branch clinical medical science.

The Brihat Trayi establishes a detailed diagnostic doctrine: etiology (Nidana), pathology stages (Samprapti), internal medicine (Kayachikitsa), toxicology (Agada Tantra), and surgical procedures (Shalya Tantra).

menu_book Charaka Samhita, Sutrasthana Ch. 1 Decoded
close The Popular Myth

“Huge raw cold salads and daily ice-cold green juices are ideal Ayurvedic cleansing foods.”

Modern wellness brands label uncooked raw leafy bowls and freezing fruit smoothies as "pure Ayurvedic detox protocols."

Status: Biologically Incompatible with Classical Agni
check What Classical Texts Actually State

Digestive fire demands warm, unctuous (Snigdha), prepared meals.

Excessive cold (Sheeta), rough (Ruksha) and raw substances directly suppress Jatharagni, triggering fermentation, bloating, and Vata aggravation. Classical texts mandate lightly seasoned, cooked foods for optimal enzymatic efficiency.

menu_book Ashtanga Hridaya, Sutrasthana Ch. 8 Decoded
close The Popular Myth

“Your Dosha is a static astrological horoscope that defines your entire identity permanently.”

10-question online quizzes label individuals permanently as "a Vata person" who must avoid hundreds of foods for life.

Status: Total Misunderstanding of Prakriti vs. Vikriti
check What Classical Texts Actually State

Doshas are dynamic physiological forces, not fixed labels.

While Prakriti represents baseline biological tendencies established at conception, Vikriti is the fluid, ever-shifting current state shaped by circadian time, weather, mental stress, sleep debt, and age.

Relevant Inquiries

Ancient knowledge. Modern questions.

Bridging the gap between millenia-old texts and today’s urban metabolic challenges.

schedule Lifestyle Integration

Can Ayurveda fit into a modern 9-to-5 desk lifestyle?

You do not need to retreat to a Himalayan ashram or cook 3-hour elaborate broths. In classical terms, 80% of systemic benefits originate from micro-habits: adhering to consistent circadian meal windows, getting direct morning photons, stopping snacking between meals, and favoring warm hydration over iced drinks.

local_fire_department Metabolism & Gut

What does Ayurveda actually say about gut digestion?

Digestion isn't merely counting calories — it is biological transformation (Agni). Classical texts identify 13 distinct types of Agni: 1 chief gastric fire (Jatharagni), 5 elemental fires (Bhutagni), and 7 cellular tissue fires (Dhatvagni). If food is not transformed completely, it turns into inflammatory metabolic waste (Ama).

tune Constitutional Science

How should we accurately understand Doshas today?

Doshas are functional physiological governing principles rather than rigid physical boxes. Vata governs electrical signaling, nerve impulses, and gut motility. Pitta directs thermogenesis, acid secretion, and enzymatic hydrolysis. Kapha orchestrates mucosal linings, structural joints, and anabolic reserve.

science Clinical Evidence

What is the modern evidence behind popular Ayurvedic practices?

Peer-reviewed clinical research increasingly corroborates Ayurvedic core tenets: time-restricted eating matches classical dinner rules; nasal oil application (Nasya) supports upper mucosal barrier defenses; and adaptogens like Withania somnifera modulate the neuroendocrine HPA-axis under acute stress.
